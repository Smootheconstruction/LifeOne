Project owner: To enable a real sign-in, send an invitation using the Supabase administrator dashboard:

  1. Sign in to the NOVA Supabase project using your administrator account.
  2. Open Authentication → Users.
  3. Choose Invite user and enter your email address.
  4. Open the invitation email and follow the account setup link. If the invitation link does not open the right page, check the Auth redirect settings in Supabase.
  5. Return to this World Connect page and use Sign in.
