Test project owner: Send invitations from NOVA-WorldConnect-Test (not live NOVA):

  1. Sign in to the NOVA Supabase project using your administrator account.
  2. Open Authentication → Users.
  3. Choose Invite user and enter your email address.
  4. Open the invitation email and follow the account setup link. If the invitation opens NOVA, use the Set your NOVA test password form; if it only shows Sign In, enter the invited email and select Forgot password.
